1. Multi-Wavelength Vapor Spectrometry

Tunable Infrared Laser Diode Absorption across Cryogenic Ullage

The vapor mass density $\rho_v$ inside the propellant tank ullage is determined by scanning infrared wavelengths across characteristic ro-vibrational absorption transitions of parahydrogen or methane. The transmitted spectral intensity follows the Beer-Lambert-Bouguer relation:

$$I(\lambda) = I_0(\lambda) \exp\left( -\int_0^L \sigma_v(\lambda, T, P) \cdot \frac{\rho_v}{M_{\text{mol}}} \, d\ell \right)$$

Where $I_0(\lambda)$ is baseline laser intensity, $\sigma_v(\lambda)$ is the pressure- and temperature-broadened absorption cross-section, $L$ is the multipass optical line-of-sight distance, and $M_{\text{mol}}$ is the molecular molar mass. Decoupling the integrated absorbance path $\tau(\lambda)$ isolates vapor mass independent of liquid globule distribution.

2. Continuous RF Cavity Modal Perturbation

Electromagnetic Waveguide Dielectric Loading

The tank's inner metallic wall forms an electromagnetic cavity resonator. Introduction of dielectric cryogenic propellant ($\varepsilon_r \approx 1.23$ for $LH_2$, $\varepsilon_r \approx 1.58$ for $LCH_4$) perturbs resonant eigenmodes $\omega_n$ according to the Slater perturbation theorem:

$$\frac{\Delta \omega_n}{\omega_{n,0}} = -\frac{\int_{V_{\text{liquid}}} (\varepsilon_r - 1) |\mathbf{E}_n|^2 \, dV + \mu_0 \int_{V_{\text{liquid}}} (\mu_r - 1) |\mathbf{H}_n|^2 \, dV}{2 \int_{V_{\text{tank}}} \varepsilon_0 |\mathbf{E}_n|^2 \, dV}$$

By sweeping hundreds of cavity eigenmodes across 100 MHz to 3 GHz, spatial modal weighting averages out local geometry, yielding an instantaneous raw liquid volume estimate $V_{\text{bulk}}$ even when fluid breaks into free-floating globules.

3. $(\lambda, m)$ Dual-State Observer & Inversion

Continuous Conservation of Total Propellant Mass

The total propellant mass $M_{\text{total}}$ is conserved as the sum of saturated liquid mass and ullage vapor mass. The state vector $\mathbf{x} = [M_{\text{liq}}, M_{\text{vap}}, T_{\text{tank}}, \dot{Q}_{\text{inleak}}]^T$ is updated continuously:

$$M_{\text{total}} = \rho_{\text{liq}}(T) \cdot \left[ V_{\text{tank}} - \frac{M_{\text{vap}}}{\rho_v(P, T, \lambda)} \right] + M_{\text{vap}}$$
$$\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k} = \hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1} + \mathbf{K}_k \left( \mathbf{z}_k - \mathbf{h}(\hat{\mathbf{x}}_{k|k-1}) \right), \quad \mathbf{z}_k = \begin{bmatrix} \lambda_{\text{peak}} \\ m_{\text{RF}} \end{bmatrix}$$

The measurement matrix $\mathbf{h}(\mathbf{x})$ reconciles optical wavelength absorption depth with bulk RF cavity shifts. Fast slosh motion induces zero net vapor density change, allowing high-frequency rejection of fluid slosh artifacts.
